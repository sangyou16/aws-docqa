Foundational requirements extend beyond a single workload. Before architecting a system, ensure requirements that influence reliability are in place.

Manage service quotas and constraints. Be aware of default quotas, monitor usage against them, and request increases before they become a limit on your workload.

Plan your network topology, including highly available connectivity, non-overlapping private IP address ranges across environments, and hub-and-spoke designs rather than many-to-many mesh connections.
