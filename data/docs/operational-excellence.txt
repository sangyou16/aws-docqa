Title: Operational excellence pillar
Source: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/operational-excellence.html
Note: Seed paraphrase written for this project; run scripts/fetch_corpus.py to replace with the page text.

The operational excellence pillar covers the ability to support development and run workloads effectively, gain insight into their operation, and continuously improve supporting processes and procedures to deliver business value.

Design principles include performing operations as code, making frequent small reversible changes, refining operations procedures frequently, anticipating failure, and learning from all operational failures.

Infrastructure as code lets teams apply the same engineering discipline used for application code to their entire environment, so changes are versioned, reviewed and repeatable rather than manual.
