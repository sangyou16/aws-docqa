Title: Operational excellence: operate

Note: Seed paraphrase written for this project; run scripts/fetch_corpus.py to replace with the page text.

Successful operation of a workload is measured by achieving business and customer outcomes. Define expected outcomes, determine how success will be measured, and identify metrics.

Utilise workload observability: collect metrics, logs and traces, build dashboards for key indicators, and create alarms that notify the right people when thresholds are breached.

Respond to events with runbooks and playbooks, define escalation paths, and communicate status through dashboards and notifications so stakeholders know what is happening.
