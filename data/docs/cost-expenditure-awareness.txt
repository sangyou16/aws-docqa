Title: Cost: expenditure and usage awareness

Understanding your organisation's costs and drivers is critical for managing cost and usage effectively and identifying cost-reduction opportunities.

Establish governance with policies and controls, such as AWS Budgets alerts that notify owners when spending crosses a threshold, and organisational account structures.

Monitor cost and usage with detailed billing data, and attribute costs to workloads, teams or projects using cost allocation tags so each owner can see what they spend.
