Detection lets you identify a potential security misconfiguration, threat or unexpected behaviour. It is an essential part of the security lifecycle and supports quality processes, legal or compliance obligations, and threat identification.

Configure service and application logging across the workload, for example AWS CloudTrail for API activity and centralised log storage, and set retention periods that meet your requirements.

Analyse logs, findings and metrics centrally, and automate responses to events. Services such as Amazon GuardDuty and AWS Security Hub aggregate findings so teams can investigate.
