Title: Security: incident response
Source: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/incident-response.html
Note: Seed paraphrase written for this project; run scripts/fetch_corpus.py to replace with the page text.

Even with mature preventive and detective controls, organisations should implement mechanisms to respond to and mitigate the potential impact of security incidents.

Prepare by identifying key personnel and external resources, developing incident management plans, and pre-provisioning access so responders can act quickly.

Run simulations, or game days, to practise incident response, and automate containment and recovery capabilities where possible. After an incident, run a post-incident analysis to learn and improve.
