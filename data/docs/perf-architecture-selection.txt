The optimal solution for a workload varies, and solutions often combine multiple approaches. Well-architected workloads use multiple solutions and enable different features to improve performance.

Learn about and understand available cloud services and features, and use guidance from your cloud provider or partners to inform architecture decisions.

Choose compute, storage, database and networking options based on data: benchmark and load test candidate options, and factor cost and performance trade-offs into the decision.
