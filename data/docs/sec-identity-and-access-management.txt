Title: Security: identity and access management
Source: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/identity-and-access-management.html
Note: Seed paraphrase written for this project; run scripts/fetch_corpus.py to replace with the page text.

Identity and access management ensures that only authorised and authenticated users and components can access your resources, and only in the ways you intend.

Grant least privilege: give identities only the permissions they need for specific actions on specific resources under specific conditions, and remove permissions that are no longer used.

Prefer temporary credentials over long-term access keys. Workloads should assume IAM roles, and federated identity providers or OpenID Connect can give CI systems short-lived credentials without storing static secrets. Require multi-factor authentication for human users.
