Title: Sustainability: alignment to demand
Source: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/alignment-to-demand.html
Note: Seed paraphrase written for this project; run scripts/fetch_corpus.py to replace with the page text.

The way users and applications consume workloads and resources can help you identify improvements that meet sustainability goals.

Scale workload infrastructure dynamically so that provisioned resources match demand, and remove or scale down idle and unused assets.

Optimise geographic placement of workloads for their users, align service levels with business needs, and use buffering or throttling to flatten demand curves.
