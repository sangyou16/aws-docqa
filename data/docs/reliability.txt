Title: Reliability pillar
Source: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/reliability.html
Note: Seed paraphrase written for this project; run scripts/fetch_corpus.py to replace with the page text.

The reliability pillar covers the ability of a workload to perform its intended function correctly and consistently when expected, including the ability to operate and test the workload through its total lifecycle.

Design principles include automatically recovering from failure, testing recovery procedures, scaling horizontally to increase aggregate workload availability, stopping guessing capacity, and managing change through automation.

Monitoring a workload for key performance indicators lets you trigger automation when a threshold is breached, so the system can notify people and recover from failure without manual intervention.
