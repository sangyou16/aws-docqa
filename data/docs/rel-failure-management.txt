Title: Reliability: failure management
Source: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/failure-management.html
Note: Seed paraphrase written for this project; run scripts/fetch_corpus.py to replace with the page text.

Failures are a given and everything will eventually fail over time. A reliable workload is designed to be aware of failures as they occur and take action to avoid impact on availability.

Back up data: identify all data that needs backing up, secure and encrypt backups, perform backups automatically, and periodically restore data to verify backup integrity.

Use fault isolation such as multiple Availability Zones to protect the workload, and plan disaster recovery with defined recovery time objective (RTO) and recovery point objective (RPO) targets.
