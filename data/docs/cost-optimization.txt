Title: Cost optimization pillar
Source: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/cost-optimization.html
Note: Seed paraphrase written for this project; run scripts/fetch_corpus.py to replace with the page text.

The cost optimization pillar covers the ability to run systems that deliver business value at the lowest price point.

Design principles include implementing cloud financial management, adopting a consumption model, measuring overall efficiency, stopping spending money on undifferentiated heavy lifting, and analysing and attributing expenditure.

With a consumption model you pay only for the computing resources you consume, and increase or decrease usage depending on business requirements, for example by stopping development environments outside working hours.
